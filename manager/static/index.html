A different view as the day unfolds.

Choose a collection for each part of the day. Each entry lasts until the next, following your TV’s local time. An entry can also start at sunrise or sunset, or a few minutes before or after; its time then moves with the seasons.

Then sync your scenery and choose Scenery → Collection → Scheduled on the TV. An empty collection falls back to other visible scenes. Older GlassOS versions, and days without a sunrise or sunset, use the sun times of the day you last synced.

Sun times location

Not checked yet

Without a place, the TV estimates sun times from its time zone’s main city, which can be off by about an hour, or more in a wide time zone or far north. Coordinates are rounded to 0.1° (about 11 km) before they leave this page, and are kept only on this Mac and the TV, never in the scenery files. Looking up from the internet address sends this Mac’s public internet address to ipinfo.io and keeps only the place, in this form; a VPN or mobile hotspot can give the wrong city.

Copy layouts, folders and display preferences between your Fire Sticks. Restoring asks you to confirm on the TV.

What travels with a backup?

App layout, folders, scenery favourites and display settings. Videos are synced separately. Accounts, permissions and the Home-button setup stay with each device.

App art, your way.

Choose an app and a PNG, JPEG or WebP image. We’ll fit it to a 400 × 225 tile. Sync the saved art to any of your TVs; themes and the custom-art switch travel with settings backups.

Saved replacements

The TV’s Custom app art switch restores original art without deleting your files. “Restore original” below removes that saved replacement from this Mac and the selected TV; other TVs keep their copies.

Forgot the code? Set a new one here: the old one is not needed, because anyone with ADB access to a TV can already reset its app lock. A new code crosses your home network as plain text and is never saved on this Mac.

Screensaver

Not checked yet

Shows your scenery when the TV is idle in any app, never while a video plays. This changes the TV’s own screensaver settings over ADB: the first time, the values it replaces are saved on this Mac so Restore can put them back. The TV is never restarted, and its sleep timers stay as they are.

Allow casting protection?

        

          This gives GlassOS Display over other apps on this TV, so its
          lock screen can cover a locked app that is cast from a phone or opened
          by Alexa or a remote button. GlassOS also uses it to show your home at
          once after Home.
        

        

          One ADB command, and nothing else changes:
          appops set io.github.abhishekkaisar.glassos.aerial SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW allow.
          To undo it, run the same command with default instead of
          allow.
